Client Requisition & Process Details

Everything you need to know about partnering with Talentechno for your engineering talent needs.

Under our guaranteed 48-Hour SLA, you receive the first 3 thoroughly vetted, interview-ready profiles within 48 business hours of requisition briefing. Every candidate has already passed our architectural and live coding benchmarks.
We provide a 90-day unconditional replacement guarantee for all permanent lateral hires. If an engineer departs or fails to meet performance expectations during their first 90 days, we source a qualified replacement immediately at zero additional fee.
Under the Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) model, Talentechno builds your dedicated engineering pod in Hyderabad, oversees local payroll, hardware provisioning, ISO 27001 security compliance, and daily operations. Once mature (typically 12-24 months), the entire entity and intellectual property can be transferred seamlessly to your direct ownership.
Our AI talent evaluations focus on practical engineering rather than surface prompt craft. We evaluate vector embeddings, RAG chunking strategies, LoRA fine-tuning benchmarks, GPU memory allocation (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM), and agentic framework orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI).
